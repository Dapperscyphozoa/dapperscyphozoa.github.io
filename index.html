Foundation — the invoice audit

The bigger one. You create one mailbox for the purpose and forward every supplier invoice to it before you pay it. Foundation checks it line by line against your own build plan and your own agreed pricing, and tells you what doesn't match — while the money is still in your account.

  • Nothing upfront, nothing monthly. Finds nothing, costs nothing.
  • We only see what you send to that one mailbox. Not your normal email, not your customers, not your accounting system.
  • Nothing is kept. Documents are audited and discarded. Your pricing is commercially sensitive and it stays that way.
  • It never buys, pays, orders or contacts anyone. It reads and it reports. Every decision stays with you.
