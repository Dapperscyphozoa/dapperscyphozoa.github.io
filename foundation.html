Send us the invoice before you pay it.

Foundation checks every invoice against your own build plan and your own agreed pricing, and tells you what doesn't match — while the money is still in your account. No licence, no subscription. You keep half of what it saves you.

How it works

1

You create a new mailbox, just for this

Something like audit@yourcompany.com.au. It exists for one purpose and you own it and control it. Foundation sees what lands in that mailbox and nothing else — not your normal email, not your customers, not your staff.

2

You send the baseline once

Your build plan, your schedule of rates, your agreed order pricing, your contract variations. That's what everything gets measured against. Your numbers, not ours.

3

You forward each invoice before you pay it

Supplier invoices, subcontractor claims, progress claims, delivery dockets, variation requests. Forward it to the mailbox and go back to work.

4

You get the audit back

Line by line: what matches your pricing, what doesn't, what was ordered but not delivered, what's been billed twice, and the exact dollar difference. With the wording to query it.

5

You decide

You query it, pay it, or hold it. Foundation never contacts your supplier, never places an order, never pays anything and never signs anything. It reads and it reports. That's the whole job.

Nothing upfront and nothing monthly. Foundation is paid only out of a difference it actually found, on an invoice you had not yet paid, measured against the pricing you supplied.

If an audit finds nothing, it costs you nothing. If it finds $1,158 like the one above, you pay $289.50, put $289.50 toward your own machine, and keep $579. Money that was about to leave and didn't.

The hardware quarter compounds. It buys a machine that ends up in your building running on your documents — fully air-gapped if you want it that way. You start by renting an audit and you finish owning the thing that does it.

Your data

  • We only ever see what you send. One mailbox, created by you, for this purpose. There is no connection to your normal email, your accounting system or your server.
  • Nothing is kept. Documents are audited and discarded. We do not retain your invoices, your pricing, your plans or your suppliers' details after the audit is returned.
  • Nothing is used to train anything. Your pricing is commercially sensitive and it stays that way.
  • You can stop at any moment by deleting the mailbox. There is nothing to cancel and no notice period.
  • Foundation never places an order or buys anything on your behalf.
  • Foundation never pays an invoice or moves any money.
  • Foundation never contacts your suppliers or anyone else in your name.
  • Foundation never signs or approves anything. Every decision stays with you.

Who it's for

Builders, civil contractors, fit-out and trade businesses running real purchase volume against a plan and a schedule of rates. If you're paying more than a handful of supplier invoices a month and nobody is checking them line by line against what was agreed, there is money sitting in them.
